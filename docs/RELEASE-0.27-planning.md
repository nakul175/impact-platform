## Delivered

Until this slice a framework's `relationships` array was accepted only empty, assumptions did not exist, a target for a locked period was refused outright (so a target amendment after close was impossible), and the status against a target was a bare comparison (`ACHIEVED`, `BELOW_TARGET`, …) with no reviewed thresholds behind an on-track / at-risk / off-track reading.

**Theory-of-change relationships (FR-PLN-002, bounded subset).** A framework revision carries typed, directed relationships between two of its own nodes: `CONTRIBUTES_TO` (the stated causal direction) or `DEPENDS_ON` (the same contribution stated from the dependent side), each with a required rationale and evidence strength (`STRONG`, `MODERATE`, `WEAK`, `UNTESTED`), the assumption records it rests on (`assumption_ids`) and optional external context. Every save refuses structural invalidity, exactly as the hierarchy rules do: a duplicate relationship identity (`DUPLICATE_RELATIONSHIP_ID`), the same link recorded twice (`DUPLICATE_RELATIONSHIP`), a self-link (`RELATIONSHIP_SELF`), an endpoint that is not a node of this framework (`RELATIONSHIP_ENDPOINT_MISSING`), a contribution from a higher result level to a lower one (`RELATIONSHIP_LEVEL_ORDER`: a result contributes to the same or a higher level, so two outcomes may contribute to one impact and alternative pathways are allowed), a causal cycle between nodes of one level (`RELATIONSHIP_CYCLE`, naming every node on the cycle) and a reference to an assumption the framework does not record (`RELATIONSHIP_ASSUMPTION_MISSING`). An untested link is a completeness warning (`UNTESTED_RELATIONSHIP`), exceptable with a reason and review date like the other warnings. Relationships are part of the reviewed revision, so they are frozen in the `framework_baseline` register with the revision they belong to, shown to the reviewer beside the candidate, returned by the targets-versus-actuals read (`framework.relationships`) and listed in the Results framework UI as readable sentences ("Outcome statement contributes to Impact statement · moderate evidence · …"). A relationship never creates a numeric sum, a deduplication rule or a causal proof: the parent result stays uncalculated until an approved method exists (FT-PLN-002).

**Assumptions and context (FR-PLN-007, bounded subset).** A framework revision carries assumption, risk and context records (`assumptions`): kind (`ASSUMPTION`, `RISK`, `CONTEXT`), the nodes it conditions (`node_ids`), statement, expected condition, evidence, owner, review date and status (`UNTESTED`, `HOLDS`, `AT_RISK`, `INVALID`). Who last assessed the status, and when (`assessed_by`, `assessed_at`), is server-owned: it is stamped when the status changes and carried unchanged otherwise, and a client that supplies it is refused by the closed draft schema; an earlier revision keeps its earlier assessment and date. Structural rules refuse a duplicate identity (`DUPLICATE_ASSUMPTION_ID`) and a link to a node outside the framework (`ASSUMPTION_NODE_MISSING`); the completeness review adds an error for a missing owner (`ASSUMPTION_INCOMPLETE`) or an owner who is not an active member (`OWNER_INELIGIBLE`), and exceptable warnings for an assumption or risk linked to no result (`UNLINKED_ASSUMPTION`), a review date that has passed (`ASSUMPTION_REVIEW_DUE`) and, when an assumption is `INVALID`, one `ASSUMPTION_INVALID` warning on **each node it conditions**, resolved by that node's owner. Invalidating an assumption therefore flags the linked results in the completeness review and blocks the revision's submission until the planner documents a reviewed exception per flagged result; the approved baseline keeps its earlier assessment and nothing recorded changes (FT-PLN-007). After approval the targets-versus-actuals row of every indicator placed on a flagged node carries `assumption_flags` (assumptions at risk or invalid), and the UI shows them beside the indicator. Assumption records are part of the reviewed revision and so of the baseline register.

**Target amendments after close (FR-IND-004, bounded subset).** A `REVISED` target that supersedes the approved target of a locked (or restatement-open) period is now accepted, reviewed independently like any target, and recorded as the next `target_binding` version; a new `ORIGINAL` target, a baseline or a revision that names nothing to supersede is still refused for a frozen period (`PERIOD_LOCKED`). The amendment applies prospectively only: the snapshot's `target_versions` are untouched, the official comparison in targets versus actuals stays against the target pinned at close (`target`, `progress`), and the amendment is shown beside it as `amended_target` with its own `amended_progress` against the same official actual and the marker `amended_after_close` (FT-IND-004: the quarter target changes from 60 to 45 after lock; the original report remains against 60 and the restated comparison is distinctly labelled). The reviewer's candidate view compares the original (`original`, the first revision of the chain, when it differs from the one being superseded), the currently effective (`superseded`) and the proposed values, and states the period state and that the change is prospective (`amendment`). The dashboard read already pins a locked period's target from the snapshot, so it is unaffected.

**Status thresholds and bands (FSD 32.2; FR-IND-003, FR-ANA-003 evidence).** A target may carry `status_thresholds`, reviewed with the target and pinned with it at close: `scheme` `ATTAINMENT_PERCENT` (a higher-is-better value target banded by attainment: on track at or above `on_track`, at risk at or above `at_risk`, otherwise off track; the standard template is 100 / 80) or `DEVIATION` (any value or range target banded by the adverse distance from the target in the indicator's unit: below a higher target, above a lower one, outside a range, never negative; on track within `on_track`, at risk within `at_risk`, otherwise off track). Mismatched or misordered thresholds are refused (`THRESHOLD_SCHEME_MISMATCH`, `THRESHOLD_ORDER`, `THRESHOLD_NEGATIVE`, `THRESHOLDS_NOT_ALLOWED` for a milestone). The pure function `planning.performance_band(value, target, stale)` computes the band from stored decimals (never displayed values); `progress()` returns it as `band` with a `band_reason` whenever no band is given: `NO_THRESHOLDS`, `NO_PRESENT_ACTUAL`, `NO_TARGET`, `MILESTONE_ASSESSMENT`, `ATTAINMENT_UNDEFINED` (zero or negative target), `STALE_ACTUAL`, `ARITHMETIC_OVERFLOW` — a missing, undefined, stale or unapproved state is visible before any performance interpretation and no template conceals it. Targets versus actuals shows the band beside the status; the dashboard's `status` block can carry it once the integrator adds `band` to the key whitelist in `dashboards.py` (S4's file; not edited here).

**UI.** The Results framework editor gains a **Theory of change** section (relationships with from/to node, type, evidence strength, rationale, external context and the assumptions each rests on) and an **Assumptions and context** section (kind, status, owner, review date, statement, expected condition, evidence, conditioned nodes); both are sent on every save, so a patch replaces the whole array as the contract defines. The framework detail and the reviewer's candidate view list relationships and assumptions with status badges. The target editor offers the threshold scheme and both bounds (defaults 100 / 80 for attainment); the target detail shows them. Targets versus actuals shows the band, the "Amended after close" marker with the prospective target and its own comparison, and the assumption flags of the indicator's nodes. The reviewer's target view shows original, currently effective and proposed values and the prospective-only note for a closed period.

## Limits

Relationships link nodes of one framework only: no cross-programme links, no separately shared summary endpoint, no graph diagram (the UI lists relationships as sentences), no relationship view editing that differs from the table (both edit the same revision), and the design's `ASSUMES` relationship type is not offered — assumptions are records a relationship names through `assumption_ids` and that condition nodes through `node_ids`. Invalidating an assumption does not create a WorkCenter task: the review happens through the completeness warning and its documented exception, and the affected rows carry `assumption_flags`; there is no notice. An assumption's history is the revision chain (each approved revision keeps its assessment); there is no per-assumption timeline read. Target amendments: only a `REVISED` target that supersedes the pinned one may follow a close; baselines and milestones of a locked period stay frozen; no explicit restatement request object exists — a restatement that chooses the amended comparison is the existing period restatement, which pins the then-current bindings when its new snapshot is created; the dashboard shows the pinned target only (no amendment marker) until S4's read is extended; no browser check covers amendments. Thresholds travel with the target version (per indicator instance and period), not with the indicator definition or a measurement-plan approval as FSD 32.2 describes, so the same bounds are re-entered per period; there is no tolerance or minimum-data-adequacy field, no threshold template library and no disaggregated or cumulative banding. Disaggregated targets (item 5 of the slice) were not started. `planning-check.mjs` was not extended (deadline); the UI additions have unit and API coverage only.
