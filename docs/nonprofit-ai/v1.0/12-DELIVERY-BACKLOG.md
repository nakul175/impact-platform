# Nonprofit AI Enablement Delivery Backlog

Edition 1.0 · 5 October 2026 · Proposed dependency order

## Planning basis

This backlog turns the BRD into reviewable work packages. It is not a commitment to dates, staffing, money or acceptance. The review branch already implements parts of R1, but it needs the remaining product controls and qualification. R2 introduces operated services; R3 is conditional on financial and operator decisions. Estimate effort only after scope and policy dependencies are resolved.

Every work package requires approved functional behavior, closed contracts, dictionary updates, tenant controls, meaningful positive/negative tests, UX evidence and a release note. Do not broaden capability ceilings or replace the original impact requirements to accelerate completion. Preserve the existing platform's deterministic official calculations and human independence.

## Proposed work packages

### WP NPA 01 Review and approve pilot scope

Resolve DEC-NPA-001, 002 and 003 for a synthetic cohort. Review all R1 BRD requirements with the product owner and representative users. Select allowed tasks and baseline quality measures, appoint named owners and approve a constrained scope. Output: signed scope, decision records, UAT participants and initial evidence plan. Covers FR-NPA-001, FR-NPA-002, FR-NPA-003, FR-NPA-026, FR-NPA-028, FR-NPA-033 and FR-NPA-035. Depends on no new financial operation.

### WP NPA 02 Qualify existing adoption workspace

Retain current profile/readiness, sourced directory, comparison, practical learning, procurement notes and saved drafts. Complete manual accessibility investigation, content version interpretation and reviewed tenant access. Inspect existing tests and run the required native/identity/container gates after owner approval of CI cost. Output: exact-commit evidence and defects, then an owner-approved release decision. Covers FR-NPA-001, FR-NPA-002, FR-NPA-003, FR-NPA-004, FR-NPA-005, FR-NPA-006, FR-NPA-007, FR-NPA-013, FR-NPA-021, FR-NPA-025, FR-NPA-027, FR-NPA-028, FR-NPA-029, FR-NPA-037, FR-NPA-038, FR-NPA-039 and FR-NPA-040. Depends on WP NPA 01 and DEC-NPA-012, 013. No supplier transactions or certification is claimed.

### WP NPA 03 Govern advisory cost and recovery

Decide provider funding and budget policy under DEC-NPA-011. Design monetary reservations, usage settlement and safe ambiguity handling if required for the chosen scope. Retain explicit consent, provider calls outside transactions, sealed replay and current authority. Do not continue credit-exhausted calls or infer a money budget from three daily attempts. Output: tested budget controls and a bounded synthetic live-provider result only when funded and authorised. Covers FR-NPA-010, FR-NPA-012, FR-NPA-023, FR-NPA-026, FR-NPA-029 and FR-NPA-035. Workbench belongs to a separate R2 slice; no autonomous action tools.

### WP NPA 04 Establish content stewardship

Appoint a curator and define review cadence, source evidence, stale-item behavior and stable learning IDs. Introduce independently reviewed content publication if operated editing is approved; preserve prior meanings for saved plans. Output: versioned source/review register, migration rules and publication tests. Covers FR-NPA-004, FR-NPA-005, FR-NPA-007, FR-NPA-018, FR-NPA-031, FR-NPA-036 and FR-NPA-038. Depends on DEC-NPA-004, 005 and 012. Reuse may copy compatible layouts with notices; no imported permissions or arithmetic.

### WP NPA 05 Operate capacity building

Approve competency rubrics, assessors and sharing under DEC-NPA-008. Build learning programme/cohort assignments, per-learner submissions, reviewed competency decisions and remediation. Output: clearly distinct self-progress and competency evidence, inaccessible cross-learner records and version-bound rubrics. Covers FR-NPA-007, FR-NPA-008, FR-NPA-009, FR-NPA-025, FR-NPA-026, FR-NPA-027 and FR-NPA-028. Depends on WP NPA 04. A lesson completion checkbox cannot create a certificate.

### WP NPA 06 Operate human advisory and support

Define service ownership, adviser conflicts, case scope, sharing, engagement terms and incident policy. Build restricted case records, explicit advisory drafts, accountable actions and closure. Add task-workbench templates only after input/output/review controls are specified and tested. Output: audited case and support journeys with named appointed advisers; no inferred procurement approval. Covers FR-NPA-010, FR-NPA-011, FR-NPA-012, FR-NPA-019, FR-NPA-032 and FR-NPA-035. Depends on DEC-NPA-003, 006, 010 and 011.

### WP NPA 07 Build supplier evidence and public listings

Choose marketplace responsibilities and verification scope. Implement private supplier submissions, independent review, expiry and an approved public snapshot. Explain editorial placement and commercial conflicts. Output: verifiable listing provenance and tenant-safe supplier portal. Covers FR-NPA-004, FR-NPA-005, FR-NPA-017, FR-NPA-018, FR-NPA-025, FR-NPA-026 and FR-NPA-031. Depends on DEC-NPA-004 and 005 and WP NPA 04. No blanket trust badge or public exposure of onboarding evidence.

### WP NPA 08 Govern procurement and engagements

Approve disclosure and procurement policies. Build versioned requests for offers, named recipient disclosure, externally evidenced responses, total-cost assumptions and independent decisions bound to exact quote revisions. Build agreed engagements and milestone acceptance as separate actions. Output: end-to-end synthetic RFQ/quote/decision/engagement evidence. Covers FR-NPA-013, FR-NPA-014, FR-NPA-015, FR-NPA-016, FR-NPA-019, FR-NPA-021, FR-NPA-025, FR-NPA-027 and FR-NPA-035. Depends on WP NPA 07 and DEC-NPA-003, 006 and 007. Draft preparation does not send supplier messages automatically.

### WP NPA 09 Connect tools and measure outcomes

Select connectors and reviewed scopes. Add credential custody, destination controls, revocation, synthetic deployment trials and failure handling. Add measured task baselines/outcomes, quality and human review effort; use existing MEL links only through governed approved source revisions. Output: approved connector-specific evidence and outcome records, rather than a savings promise. Covers FR-NPA-021, FR-NPA-022, FR-NPA-024, FR-NPA-029, FR-NPA-034, FR-NPA-035 and FR-NPA-036. Depends on DEC-NPA-002, 014 and 016 and an approved pilot delivery scope.

### WP NPA 10 Complete lifecycle and portability

Approve retention and exit rights. Build separately permitted exports with versions/context and audited scope; integrate privacy removal through the existing authorised mechanism and document backup limits. Output: tenant-safe export/removal tests, support runbooks and exit evidence. Covers FR-NPA-026, FR-NPA-030, FR-NPA-032 and FR-NPA-040. Depends on DEC-NPA-009 and 010. Retention durations must be supplied by policy, not guessed in schema defaults.

### WP NPA 11 Conditional purchasing and payments

After DEC-NPA-004, 007 and 015, define which party moves money and owns reconciliation, refunds and disputes. Design approved purchase bindings, external idempotency, signed event verification and immutable reconciled records. Output: synthetic end-to-end payment/recovery qualification against the chosen model before money moves. Covers FR-NPA-020, FR-NPA-023, FR-NPA-025, FR-NPA-027, FR-NPA-029 and FR-NPA-035. Depends on WP NPA 08. No payment provider or merchant model is selected in this edition.

## Completion and reporting

Report requirements as proposed, implemented locally, qualified in a named environment or accepted by the appointed human, with dates and evidence. Do not collapse these into one percentage. The original platform ledger remains 113 PARTIAL, 194 PENDING and zero accepted out of 307; this extension has its own 36 business and 40 functional requirements. No acceptance is created by the documentation suite.

For each reviewed slice, record exact commit, changed contracts/migrations/capabilities, executed test evidence, unresolved defects, operational dependencies and owner sign-off. A work package can be deferred only through an explicit scoped decision and traceability update. The next useful owner action is to review the BRD and resolve the initial-pilot choices; implementation can then proceed in small slices against the approved behavior.
