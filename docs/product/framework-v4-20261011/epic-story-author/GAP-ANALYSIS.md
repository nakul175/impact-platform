## What needed improvement

1. **Epics were primarily inventory summaries.** Their saved descriptions counted children/releases and linked strategy, but did not sufficiently state the coherent capability, explicit handoffs, riskiest assumption, comparative priority or outcome-learning boundary. The new54 authored contracts give each area a domain-specific problem, capability outcome, handoff, evidence contract and falsifiable question. They retain the existing canonical parent and child population.

2. **Story plans were technically richer than their business decisions.** Every story had scope, recorded code paths, decisions, tasks and scenario plans. Their desired benefit did not have a comparable observation plan or a clear priority alternative/opportunity cost. The new368 annotations bind those plans to the exact source persona and desired benefit, compare delivery with an alternative or defer, and expose unknown baselines, size attribution and review windows. These are hypotheses and recommended sequencing, not observed research or native priority changes.

3. **Criterion verification needed a stronger authoritative-evidence contract.** Existing scenario plans largely copied Then assertions and proposed a test level. The new1,328 criterion records retain the exact original scenario text/hash, assign stable `<story>-AC-<ordinal>` identities and annotate authoritative state/response/audit/receipt or approved review evidence. Original rejection summaries and every numeric/source threshold remain intact. No original Given, When, Then or criterion is narrowed or replaced.

4. **Ready, evidence, acceptance and value needed separate states.** Written plans, merged code, Linear status and framework checks cannot establish all of these. The additions pin the actual team Ready/Done documents and keep source decisions unresolved until actual evidence/authority exists. No supplemental item becomes Ready, Done or accepted from this refinement. Four current review stories remain in review.

5. **Cross-cutting safety had to remain unsampled.** AI use is explicit in the product. An individual story with no obvious model call is not automatically exempt from derived-content, privacy or provider obligations. Story annotations identify direct source AI signals and require factual scoped applicability review. Unknown rights, datasets, critical-slice targets, configuration, fallback, retention or legal decisions stay unresolved. This author did not make external calls or generate new provider/real-data authority.

6. **Outcome language needed careful interpretation.** `US-DX-03` says baseline metrics let the organisation “prove what changed”; preserve that source text, but qualify the actual observation as comparable baseline/follow-up, not causal attribution. `US-IM-02` and value-case stories distinguish realised observations from forecast ROI. A fit score (`US-DC-02`) is explained evidence, not proven user confidence. `US-VC-03` export does not demonstrate quicker board approval. `US-CM-09` does not establish an actual supplier contract or DPA merely because a form is written.

## Recommended order without altering source commitments

Keep the existing four next-sprint candidate stories and their24 original task cores. The manual diagnosis (`US-DX-01a`) tests stable problem identity and agreed priorities before adding conversational intake. A locked baseline (`US-DX-03`) gives comparable follow-up a fixed reference. Budgeted jobs (`FR-AI-016a`) and timeouts/partial drafts (`FR-AI-016b`) require the policy and data/destination controls to be evidenced before model execution. This is a proposal: no sprint, assigned person, date, provider or spend is approved.

For discovery, use one isolated source-positive/refusal walkthrough per selected capability before recruiting an actually authorised representative population. A consequential disclosure, lost work, natural-person independence bypass or incorrect official result stops the affected commitment. Source thresholds are retained; any new outcome threshold, sample, period or assigned evaluator remains an explicit decision before dependent commitment.
