## Product and quality objectives

Qualify meaningful nonprofit work: plan and collect evidence, independently review it, calculate official results, publish governed reports and use the AI enablement workspace with an honest manual/deterministic fallback. Judge durable intended outcomes and absence of prohibited effects, not a success toast, queued dispatch or number of green tests.

Tenant isolation, current authority, distinct natural-person decisions, immutable approved/revision/audit history and deterministic decimal official arithmetic are protected invariants. AI drafts cannot grant authority, change official results, purchase, contact suppliers or make consequential decisions automatically. Critical violations block the affected release regardless of average quality or pass rate. Unknown applicability is unresolved, not an exclusion.

This is a development product. Real data, supported platform/language commitments, accepted business outcomes, independent specialist approvals and release readiness are not established by these documents. Live provider mechanics, held-out AI quality, external security assessment, manual accessibility, representative performance and deployed recovery require their own evidence.

## Risk and test portfolio

Use the original story/AC and failure mode to select the smallest meaningful layer, then add affected shared controls and critical journeys. Proposed conservative high/critical families are disclosure, authority/independence bypass, acknowledged-data loss, official-number corruption, history alteration and unauthorized external/AI action. The executor and relevant reviewer must confirm risk tier, impact/likelihood basis, scope and required cases before a qualification claim; the existing threat register is not a general approved likelihood register.

For future reviewed risk records, carry the package's `standards/RISK-CLASSIFICATION.md` rule explicitly: impact and likelihood each use 1–4, score is their product, **impact 4 is Critical regardless of score**, High is score 9–16 or material irreversible action, Medium 4–8 and Low 1–3. Unknown likelihood needs a conservative provisional rating, stated basis and resolution plan; it is not invented observed frequency. Escalate for sensitive data, vulnerable users, broad/hard-to-detect effects, external actions or unvalidated AI. Record inherent and evidence-based residual risk separately; defect severity and scheduling priority remain different decisions. Critical requires threat/abuse/fallback/isolation checks and independent specialist review; High adds relevant boundary/integration/E2E/recovery evidence; Medium needs affected interfaces and regression; Low permits focused verification or review proportionate to the change. These are proposed adoption rules, not newly approved ratings or a waiver of contractual/legal/accessibility requirements.
